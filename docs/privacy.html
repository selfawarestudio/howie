Howie — Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 17, 2026

Howie is a personal, non-commercial SMS notification service that sends daily New York Mets game recaps and previews to opted-in friends and family.

Information we collect

We collect only the mobile phone numbers of recipients who have explicitly opted in to receive messages.

How we use information

Phone numbers are used solely to send the daily Mets game update messages you requested. We do not use your number for advertising, resale, or unrelated purposes.

Data sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share recipient phone numbers with third parties for marketing. Messages are delivered via Twilio, our SMS provider, which processes data according to its own privacy policy.

Opt-out

You may stop receiving messages at any time by replying STOP to any message. Reply HELP for assistance.

Data retention

Phone numbers are retained only while you remain an opted-in recipient. You may request removal by replying STOP or contacting the service operator directly.

Contact

Questions about this policy: reply HELP to any Howie message, or open an issue at github.com/selfawarestudio/howie.
